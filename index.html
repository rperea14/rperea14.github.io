🔬 Academic & Scientific Deep Dive

The Ph.D. Neuroimaging Experiment

Investigating how cardiorespiratory fitness and exercise modulate white matter microstructural integrity in the aging and Alzheimer's brain.

University of Kansas • Bioengineering Ph.D. Dissertation

"The impact of exercise on brain's white matter in aging and Alzheimer's disease"

Using high-resolution Diffusion Tensor Imaging (DTI) and tract-based spatial statistics, this research quantified how aerobic exercise interventions preserve axonal diameter, myelin density, and cholinergic pathways (specifically the fornix and corpus callosum) to slow down cognitive decline.

This experimental foundation later expanded during my Postdoctoral Fellowship at Harvard Medical School & Massachusetts General Hospital, leading to multi-modal PET/MRI biomarkers published in Nature Neuroscience and Neurobiology of Aging.

🔬 Key Neuroimaging Insights & Methods

  • ✓ DTI Tractography: Quantified Fractional Anisotropy (FA), Mean Diffusivity (MD), and Radial Diffusivity (RD).
  • ✓ Fornix & Memory: Proved that fornix microstructural integrity synergizes with cortical amyloid burden to accelerate memory loss.
  • ✓ Tau Spreading: Demonstrated that white matter tract breakdown is an antecedent pathway for tau propagation in preclinical Alzheimer's.
  • ✓ $5.3M NIH Portfolio: Contributed to the Harvard Aging Brain Study (HABS) longitudinal cohort.

MedicAI Platform

MedicAI brings ambient voice intelligence to clinical consultations. It streams live audio via WebSockets, uses Deepgram Nova-3 for millisecond acoustic diarization between doctor and patient, and auto-generates structured SOAP notes with CIE-10 (ICD-10) diagnosis auto-coding.
